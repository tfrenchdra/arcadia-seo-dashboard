Executive Summary

Arcadia dropped from 44 to 32 tracked top-10 keywords between Oct 29 and Nov 2 while competitors held steady. A spam link burst hit the homepage the same week. Arcadia still beats TheKey and Caring.com and is still well ahead of January. Visiting Angels and Home Instead lead locally. Every competitor has 3x to 50x more referring domains.

Strategic Threats & Insights

Findings from the data in this snapshot, ordered by impact.

criticalArcadia lost 12 top-10 keywords in four days: 44 on Oct 29 to 32 on Nov 2, 2026
Top-10 keywords held between 41 and 50 through October, then fell to 32 starting Oct 30. Competitors did not move over the same days, so this is not a tracking outage. It landed during a stretch of extremely volatile Google SERPs in late October. A live Google check confirms it: reduce cost of elder care went from #1 to outside the top 100 while the page is live and indexable. Blog posts took the biggest hit (dehydration, employee vs contractor, vision loss, what to expect from a caregiver), plus home care san rafael ca (#1).

Recommendation: Check Search Console for manual actions, security issues and a crawl or indexing change on Oct 29 to 30. Re-check rankings on Nov 9 before changing any page.

criticalA spam link burst hit the homepage at the same time: 244 new referring domains from Oct 27 to Nov 2
Referring domains jumped from 394 on Oct 2 to 655. 244 of the new domains are throwaway .shop, .store, .site, .space and .online pages using one anchor: "Expert SEO Links and Backlinks for arcadiahomecare.com Ranking Growth". All are dofollow and all point at the homepage. The timing overlaps the ranking drop. That is a correlation, not proof. Google usually ignores links like these.

Recommendation: Confirm the cluster in a second tool, then disavow it at the domain level. It is clear-cut spam, so it meets the evidence bar.

criticalOctober organic sessions fell -27% to 2,018, the lowest month since tracking began
GA4 shows 2,018 organic sessions in Oct 2026, vs 2,764 in Sep 2026 and 2,525 a year earlier. Blog traffic fell -43% and the homepage -28%. The new location pages went the other way: 98 to 212 sessions. Most of the decline predates the Oct 30 ranking drop. Google released a spam update early in October, and SERPs were extremely volatile late in the month.

Recommendation: Compare October clicks and impressions in Search Console against the early-October spam update and the late-October volatility to see which pages moved first.

opportunityYear-to-date gain still stands: 18 top-10 keywords on Jan 1, 32 now
Even after the drop, Arcadia holds 32 top-10 terms vs 18 on Jan 1. It still outranks TheKey (11) and Caring.com (2) on the shared set. New metro pages are moving: in home care services San Jose ranks #6 and home care services Phoenix ranks #20.

Recommendation: Keep the location-page cadence while the drop is investigated.

criticalThe metro terms with real volume are still mostly unranked: 4,630 searches/mo vs 1,070 for terms Arcadia ranks on
Arcadia ranks on 22 of 58 tracked location keywords. Those carry 1,070 monthly searches. The 36 unranked terms carry 4,630, led by home care services phoenix az (590), home care services san francisco (590), san francisco home care services (590), home care service phoenix (480), home care services oakland (320).

Recommendation: Build or retarget metro pages for San Francisco, Phoenix, Oakland, San Diego and Sacramento. Target the metro, not the office city.

criticalVisiting Angels owns the local set: 79 top-10 terms vs Arcadia's 32
Visiting Angels ranks top 10 on 79 of 234 tracked keywords and outranks Arcadia on 103 of them. On location terms it holds 34 of 58 top-10 spots vs Arcadia's 12 of 58. Its franchise model gives every territory a dedicated page and a GBP-linked URL.

Recommendation: Prioritize location pages where Visiting Angels ranks top 3 and Arcadia sits on page 2. Pair each page with GBP linking to that page, not the homepage.

warningHome Instead is the second local threat: 49 top-10 terms, ahead of Arcadia on 63 keywords
Home Instead holds 49 top-10 terms, down from 94 on May 7. Arcadia is ahead on 33 shared keywords and behind on 63. Its service-area URL pattern (/service-area/manhattan-ny/) ranks for metro terms beyond the office city.

Recommendation: Mirror the service-area pattern: one page per metro served, not per office address.

warningAI Overview citations fell from 5 to 2 of 10 tracked queries
On Oct 2 Arcadia was cited on 5 queries. Today it is cited on home care vs assisted living, what to expect from a caregiver. It lost dehydration, reduce cost of senior care, employee vs contractor and vision loss, the same posts that fell out of organic results. It gained home care vs assisted living, where it now ranks #2. Visiting Angels is cited on 2, Home Instead on 0, Caring.com on 0, TheKey on 0.

Recommendation: AIO citations follow organic rank here. Fix the organic drop first, then refresh the four lost posts.

criticalAuthority gap: Domain InLink Rank 52 vs 82 to 89 for every competitor
Arcadia's score slipped from 53 to 52 in October. Its referring-domain count rose, but only because of the spam burst. TheKey has 1,327 referring domains, Visiting Angels 7,471, Home Instead 8,663. Arcadia's best links are community sponsorships (Walk to End Alzheimer's, Council on Aging, HDSA, Marin HHS).

Recommendation: Turn sponsorships into a link program. Ask each sponsored event and agency partner to link to the matching location page.

warningAI search is still small: 196 sessions Jan to Oct 2026, 0.7% of search
AI assistants sent 196 sessions to 31 pages this year, vs 28,798 organic. The share climbed from 0.4% in January to about 0.9% in September and October.

Recommendation: Track it monthly. Do not shift budget from local SEO to AEO on this volume.

opportunityInformational gap: TheKey owns Medicare, live-in and 24-hour care questions
TheKey ranks #1 for medicare home health (22.4K/mo), 24 hour care (14.8K), live in home care (10.8K) and live in care (5.4K). Arcadia does not rank for any of them. These are decision-stage questions families ask before calling an agency.

Recommendation: Build a payment-and-care-types cluster: Medicare coverage, live-in vs 24-hour, respite care, cost of home care in CA, AZ and TX.

warningWrong listings still point at Arcadia
MapQuest lists Arcadia in Pittsburgh PA, Birdeye ties the domain to a hospice in Ocala FL, and YP ties it to "Supple Senior Care" in San Francisco. The older spam anchor "Start Own SEO business" is still on 56 referring domains. Wrong NAP data confuses local entity signals.

Recommendation: Fix the listings. Add the older spam cluster to the same disavow review.

warningNational traffic figures are New York-skewed. Read them as scale, not as Arcadia's market
SE Ranking's US estimates credit Home Instead, TheKey, Visiting Angels and Caring.com with their largest pages in Manhattan, Brooklyn and North Jersey. Their national totals say little about CA, AZ or TX searches. The tracked keyword set is the fair head-to-head.

Recommendation: Add city-level tracking for the 9 CA branch metros in SE Ranking so competitor comparisons reflect Arcadia's actual markets.
